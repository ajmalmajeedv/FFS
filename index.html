Design philosophy and research

Designed so people want to stay.

We combine user behaviour psychology with environmental data to shape built forms that invite people to stay longer, inspired by nature.

User behaviour psychology

How people move, pause, gather and choose where to sit. We design for dwell time and the small decisions that make a place feel easy to be in.

Environmental data

Sun, shade, wind and temperature are analysed and tested before the design is fixed, so spaces stay comfortable when people actually use them.

Inspired by nature

Natural systems set the pattern: flowing contours, layered planting and sheltered edges. Built form follows how landscape already works.

Measure

Shadow, acoustic and thermal studies show where people will actually be comfortable.

Predict

Simulations run during design, so shading, materials and planting are adjusted before construction.

Justify

Evidence supports coordination with your team, clients and authorities, and cuts revisions and approval delays.

Economic modelling

Design to the budget, by construction.

On a national housing parks commission for Aldar, the client set a budget of AED 375 per sqm of open space, kept low maintenance. We took species prices from the local nursery and built them into the design workflow as a live rule.

01

Client brief

AED 375 per sqm of open space, kept low maintenance.

02

Nursery pricing

Species prices taken from the local nursery.

03

Parametric rule

Prices sit inside the Grasshopper and Rhino workflow as a live constraint. It places only the trees and hardscape the budget allows.

04

Result

A design that meets AED 375 per sqm by construction, not by later cost cutting.
